# Tesla V100 measurements, October 2026

What a `70` build ([Tesla V100](../build-linux.md#tesla-v100-sm_70)) does on one Tesla
V100-SXM2-16GB: which models fit, the engine's prompt and decode rates per model and KV format,
the server's rates with speculation, and llama.cpp on the same card. The setup is at the
[end](#how-these-were-measured).

### Several requests at once

Total generated tokens per second, Ternary Bonsai 2, `rk8v4`, the short-chat prompts in turn,
512 tokens each:

| speculation | 1 at once | 2 at once | 4 at once | 8 at once |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| none | 72.0 | 129.7 | 215.5 | 275.5 |
| MTP, 3 drafts | 99.7 | 153.0 | 172.3 | 191.1 |
| DFlash2, 5 drafts | 91.7 | 108.2 | 113.9 | - |

MTP pays on the V100 up to two requests at once, DFlash2 at one. From four, the batched verify
rounds leave the T2 integer route (one to eight columns) for the FP16 tensor-core one, and
speculation decodes slower than plain decoding. Eight DFlash2 lanes of 16,384 tokens do not fit
16 GB; that row ran with `--max-concurrency 4 --kv-capacity 65536`.

## Known limits

- With `--vision` the Volta build plans 2.06 GiB of workspace for a 16,384-token image (other
  targets: under 827 MiB). Its wide projections stage FP16 activations and FP32 product planes.
- Over the same image the CPU vision encoder and the device one put the first answer token's two
  likeliest choices 0.27 apart in log probability on IQ3_S. The check that compares them allows
  0.25.
- Prefix reuse of a multimodal prompt with MTP did not reproduce the first run's second token on
  IQ3_S, so a stop on that token was not reached (`ninfer_qwen3_5_prefix_real_test`). The cause is
  not established.
- The real-artifact tests that need more than 16 GB (`dflash2_real`, `hybrid_prefix_real`,
  `dflash_prefill_real`) or another artifact (`moe_real`, `dflash_real`) do not run on this card.

| experts | prefill tok/s | decode tok/s | expert cache hits |

The device holds the dense weights and an expert cache of about 7,600 slots. Host and disk experts

## Against the RTX 3090

The same artifacts and protocol on the RTX 3090 of the [reference page](reference-2026-09.md)
(Ternary Bonsai 2 at 390 W) and of the [IQ3_S page](../gguf.md#measurements) (420 W), decode
tok/s:

| model, KV, speculation | request | V100 | RTX 3090 | V100 / 3090 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bonsai 2, `rk4v4`, none | short chat | 74.5 | 93.8 | 0.79 |
| Bonsai 2, `rk4v4`, none | after 32,768 | 64.5 | 83.7 | 0.77 |
| Bonsai 2, `rk4v4`, none | after 131,072 | 46.2 | 64.4 | 0.72 |
| Bonsai 2, `rk4v4`, MTP 3 | short chat | 122.8 | 173.4 | 0.71 |
| Bonsai 2, `rk4v4`, MTP 3 | after 131,072 | 46.1 | 96.5 | 0.48 |
| Bonsai 2, `rk4v4`, DFlash2 5 | short chat | 117.8 | 200.8 | 0.59 |
| Bonsai 2, `rk4v4`, DFlash2 5 | after 131,072 | 40.6 | 97.5 | 0.42 |
| Bonsai 2, `rk8v4`, none | eight requests at once | 275.5 | 546.4 | 0.50 |
| Bonsai 2, `rk8v4`, MTP 3 | eight requests at once | 191.1 | 550.8 | 0.35 |
| Bonsai 2, `rk8v4`, DFlash2 5 | four requests at once | 113.9 | 334.1 | 0.34 |
| IQ3_S, `rk8v4`, none | short chat | 44.1 | 59.9 | 0.74 |
| IQ3_S, `rk8v4`, MTP 3 | short chat | 74.9 | 108.7 | 0.69 |

Prompt time of Bonsai 2 is 2.6-2.7x the RTX 3090's at 1,024 and 8,192 tokens and 3.6x at 131,072.

The two cards read memory at about the same rate (900 against 936 GB/s), so one request's
decode stays within 0.7-0.8 of the RTX 3090. The rest of the gap has four causes:

- **Prompts.** Volta multiplies integers only on its CUDA cores. Wide products therefore
  dequantize the weights to FP16 for cuBLAS, about 92 TFLOPS, where the RTX 3090 runs its integer
  tensor cores. Prompt attention past 32K tokens widens the gap further.
- **Verify rounds and batches.** Several columns cost more compute per weight byte. The T2 integer
  route covers up to eight columns. Wider rounds and batches run FP16 tensor-core products, which
  spend most of their instructions widening codes.
- **Decode attention at depth.** The Volta kernel reads each key row with per-lane loads. At
  131K keys it takes about 8 ms of a token's time, against about 3 ms for the bytes it reads.
- **IQ3_S's grid quantizations** (IQ3_S, IQ3_XXS, IQ2_S) are bound by decoding, not by reading.
  A 5,120 x 17,408 matrix takes about 70 us in any of them: 485-550 GB/s, against 704-785 GB/s
  for its IQ4_XS and Q4_K matrices.

## How these were measured

- **Card.** One Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB at its 300 W limit (80 SMs, 1,530 MHz, HBM2 at 877 MHz; a
  streaming read reaches 856 GB/s), in a container on a host with a Xeon E5-2683 v4 (64 threads)
  and 76 GB of RAM, Ubuntu 24.04, driver 580.178.04. A Tesla P4 in the same host was not used.
- **Build.** `-DCMAKE_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES=70`, CUDA 12.9.86, GCC 13.3, Release. The
  device route profile is the built-in one.
- **llama.cpp.** `f2cc728` (2026-10-11), CUDA 12.9 for `sm_70`, `llama-bench -ngl 99 -fa 1 -r 2`,
  the GGUFs the artifacts were converted from.
- **Engine rates.** `ninfer_bench --max-ctx 4096` (17,408 for the 16K rows); one warm-up run.
- **Server.** `ninfer-serve <artifact> --max-context W --kv-capacity W --max-concurrency 1
  --gdn-state-fp16` with the KV format and speculation of the row, everything else at its default;
  [`tools/bench/refbench.py`](../../tools/bench/refbench.py) as on the
  [reference page](reference-2026-09.md#how-these-were-measured): greedy, thinking off,
  non-streaming; short chat is the mean decode rate of five 512-token answers, a depth row a cold
  document of that many tokens (PG-19, WikiText and this repository's text) and an answer of about
  400 words. Several requests at once: `--max-concurrency 8 --max-context 16384 --kv-capacity
  131072`, `rk8v4`, the short-chat prompts in turn.
- **Largest context.** `--max-context` = `--kv-capacity` bisected on whether the server starts.
